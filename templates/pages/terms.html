Registering

Registration is free. One person registers once per event, using their own details. A pass belongs to the person named on it and cannot be passed on.

At the event

Bring your pass and your college ID. The door team may check your ID against your pass. Follow KL University's campus rules and the instructions of the event team.

Cancelling

You can cancel your registration at any time before the event starts, from your pass. {{ CLUB_NAME }} may change or cancel an event, and will email everyone registered if it does.
