04FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Write to {{ CLUB_CONTACT_EMAIL }} and the student team will reply.

Does it cost anything?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
No. Every {{ CLUB_NAME }} event on this portal is free to join.
Who can take part?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
Most events are for KL students, and some are open to students from other colleges. Each event page says who can join. Events marked KL only ask you to verify your KL email once.
Do I need a team?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
Many events, hackathons especially, are team events. The event page and its registration form tell you whether you need a team.
What do winners get?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
Winning teams take home funds or certificates. Each event page explains what is on offer for that event.
How do I sign in?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
Type your email and we send you a sign in code. There is no password. KL students should use their KL email, so they are verified as KL in the same step.
My code hasn't arrived. What now?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
Codes can take a few minutes. Check your Spam or Junk folder, then ask for a new code. If it still doesn't come, write to {{ CLUB_CONTACT_EMAIL }}.
Can I cancel my registration?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
Yes. Open your pass and choose Cancel my registration any time before the event starts. Your seat goes to someone else.
How do I get in on the day?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
Open your pass on your phone, or a screenshot of it, and show the QR at the door. A screenshot works without signal, and you can also read out your code.
Do events give SIL points?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
Some do. Look for the SIL points tag on the event card, and the details on the event page.
Can I just walk in without registering?{% include "ui/icon.html" with name="plus" %}
The door team can sometimes add walk ins when seats are free, but registering is the only way to be sure of your seat.
